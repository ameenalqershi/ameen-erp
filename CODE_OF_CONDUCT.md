# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make WOW ERP a respectful, technically rigorous community for contributors of all experience levels. Participation is welcome regardless of background, language, identity, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, give actionable review feedback, credit others' contributions, respect different opinions, and keep discussion related to the project. English and Arabic are both welcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, intentionally disruptive conduct, publishing private credentials or customer data, and retaliation against people who raise concerns are not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

For non-security conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately via their GitHub profile rather than creating a public issue containing sensitive information. Maintainers will review context, keep reports limited to those who need to know, and may warn, restrict, or remove participants based on severity. If the report concerns a maintainer, request a private discussion with another trusted maintainer when one exists.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this public issue workflow.

## Enforcement scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and project spaces. Maintainers may moderate project spaces to protect participants and constructive work.
